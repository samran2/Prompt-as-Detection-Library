# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a useful place for people to learn, collaborate, and
improve defensive security tooling. Treat contributors and users with respect,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal circumstances.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence constructively. Explain disagreements without
  attacking the person raising them.
- Give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality. Share examples only when you have the
  right to share them, and prefer synthetic security data.
- Stay within the agreed scope of any security testing.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Neither is
publishing someone else's confidential information or credentials.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project-managed discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other community spaces, including conduct while officially
representing the project.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request changes in behavior, restrict
participation, or exclude a participant when needed to protect the community.
They should consider context, explain decisions privately when practical, and
handle reports with care. Information should be shared only with people who
need it to assess or address a report.

## Reporting concerns

**Before public collaboration begins, the owner must appoint a conduct contact
and publish a verified private reporting route here.** No contact address is
currently configured. Until then, use an existing private channel to the owner
if one is available; do not post private report details in public project
spaces.

Reports should describe what happened, where it happened, and the information
needed to understand the concern. If a maintainer is involved, ask for an
uninvolved reviewer when one is available. A report may be made on behalf of
another affected person with their permission.
