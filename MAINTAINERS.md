# Maintainers

This is the authoritative public roster for repository maintenance roles. It is
kept deliberately factual: CODEOWNERS entries, GitHub permissions and past
contributions do not by themselves make someone a maintainer or qualified
reviewer.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- apply the governance, conduct, security, provenance and licensing policies;
- keep changes focused and require evidence proportionate to their risk;
- distinguish automated checks from human approval and from real detection
  validation;
- disclose conflicts of interest and recuse when independent review is needed;
- grant repository access only after the documented onboarding checks; and
- keep this roster and CODEOWNERS accurate when responsibilities change.

Maintainers do not certify generated detections as production-ready merely by
merging them. No maintainer may waive third-party rights, invent contributor
sign-offs or describe an unavailable review as completed.

## Joining or leaving

The reviewer and maintainer criteria, nomination process, access checklist and
offboarding steps are in
[Maintainer onboarding](docs/maintainer-onboarding.md). A public roster change
records a role decision; access is granted or removed separately using least
privilege.

There are no emeritus or former maintainers recorded in this repository. If that
changes, historical names may be listed only with the person's consent and
without implying current authority.

For public project questions, use the routes in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Report
vulnerabilities through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
