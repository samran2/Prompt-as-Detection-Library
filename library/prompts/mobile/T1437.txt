Behavior to investigate: Adversaries may communicate using application layer protocols to avoid detection/network filtering by blending in with existing traffic. Commands to the mobile device, and often the results of those commands, will be embedded within the protocol traffic between the mobile device and server. 

Adversaries may utilize many different protocols, including those used for web browsing, transferring files, electronic mail, or DNS.
