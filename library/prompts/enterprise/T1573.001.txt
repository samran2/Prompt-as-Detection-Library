Task: Propose detection logic, required telemetry, false positives, and bounded test cases.
Output target: Platform-neutral. This is an output instruction, not a verified integration.

Behavior to investigate: Adversaries may employ a known symmetric encryption algorithm to conceal command and control traffic rather than relying on any inherent protections provided by a communication protocol. Symmetric encryption algorithms use the same key for plaintext encryption and ciphertext decryption. Common symmetric encryption algorithms include AES, DES, 3DES, Blowfish, and RC4.
