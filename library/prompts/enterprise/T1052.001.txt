Task: Propose detection logic, required telemetry, false positives, and bounded test cases.
Output target: Platform-neutral. This is an output instruction, not a verified integration.

Behavior to investigate: Adversaries may attempt to exfiltrate data over a USB connected physical device. In certain circumstances, such as an air-gapped network compromise, exfiltration could occur via a USB device introduced by a user. The USB device could be used as the final exfiltration point or to hop between otherwise disconnected systems.
